The Buydown
Verdict

Points are a bet on how long you keep the loan. Most buyers place it blind. Price the bet — permanent points against a seller-paid 2-1 buydown — and take only the side that pays you back.

I · The Loan

A seller credit is not free money until you spend it on the right thing. Section III settles where it earns most.

II · The Bet

One point = 1% of the loan, paid at closing.

The classic quote is 0.25% per point. If your lender offers less, the bet gets worse fast — enter the real number.

Until you sell or refinance. A refi kills a buydown exactly like a sale does. Be honest — most loans die young.

III · Spend the Seller Credit: Permanent vs 2-1

A 2-1 buydown cuts your rate 2% in year one and 1% in year two, then it's gone. Its cost is simply the payments someone else makes for you — which is why sellers love funding it and why it only wins if you leave or refinance early.

VI · The Method

1. Points only pay if you outlive the break-even month. Sell or refinance one month early and you funded the lender's margin.

2. Rate cut per point is negotiable and varies by lender and day. A 0.125% cut per point nearly doubles your break-even — always enter the quoted cut, never the folklore.

3. Seller credit changes the bet: points bought with the seller's money can win even on a short hold, and a 2-1 is pure gift if the seller funds it — compare both before you sign, not after.
